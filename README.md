# sAIs – Smart Autonomous Intelligent System
## Engineering System Design & Proposal Presentation

**Project Name:** sAIs  
**Full Name:** Smart Autonomous Intelligent System  
**Tagline:** Your parking attendant, reimagined.  
**Category:** Smart Mobility & Autonomous Parking  
**Project Status:** Baseline – Planning Phase  
**Team Size:** 6 Members  
**Deployment Context:** Indoor Shopping Mall Parking Facility  
**Primary Benchmark:** HL Robotics PARKIE  

---

## Technical Stack
- **Presentation Framework:** Reveal.js (v5.x)
- **Frontend Core:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Diagrams & Visuals:** Inline custom SVG schematics (architecture, robot mechanics, facility topologies)
- **Server:** Built-in Node.js static server (`server.js`) with zero third-party runtime server dependencies

---

## Complete Slide Structure (16 Slides)
1. **Slide 1 – Title & Hero:** Project identity, metadata, indoor AGV blueprint hero graphic.
2. **Slide 2 – The Problem:** Analysis of conventional shopping mall parking inefficiencies & comparison table.
3. **Slide 3 – Introducing sAIs:** Core value proposition (*Finding* vs. *Handling*) & 5-subsystem unified model.
4. **Slide 4 – The Customer Journey:** 5-stage sequential user experience from pre-arrival to retrieval.
5. **Slide 5 – System Architecture:** Detailed multi-tier diagram (User Interface, Central Orchestration, Robot Edge).
6. **Slide 6 – Intelligent Parking Management:** Sensing array, space allocation algorithm, and indoor facility bay topology.
7. **Slide 7 – Autonomous Robotic Valet:** 6-stage vehicle handling sequence & tire-cradling mechanical schematic.
8. **Slide 8 – Hybrid Multi-Robot Fleet Management:** Centralized fleet scheduling paired with autonomous local edge control.
9. **Slide 9 – Mobile Application:** High-fidelity smartphone mockup concepts for the 6 core customer workflows.
10. **Slide 10 – AI & Predictive Analytics:** 5-stage data pipeline, demand curve forecasting, and arrival window advice.
11. **Slide 11 – Smart Services & Future Expansion:** EV charging integration, automated car wash, and security monitoring.
12. **Slide 12 – Technology Benchmarks & Case Study:** HL Robotics PARKIE and STAN analysis, vendor integrity principles.
13. **Slide 13 – Implementation Strategy:** Phased 6-step engineering roadmap from feasibility to commercial rollout.
14. **Slide 14 – Project Management & Team Structure:** 6 team members mapped to their charter deliverables.
15. **Slide 15 – Project Feasibility, Risks & Resources:** Technical & operational risk matrix, CAPEX vs. OPEX framework.
16. **Slide 16 – Conclusion:** Summary of the 5 core pillars, closing identity, and review committee Q&A.

## How to Run & Present

### Option 1: Using the Node.js Server (Recommended)
1. In your terminal, run:
   ```bash
   npm start
   ```
2. Open your browser to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```

### Option 2: Direct Local Browser Open
Double-click [index.html](file:///d:/NTI%20presentation/index.html) to open directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
