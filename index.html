Operational Context • Problem Statement

Conventional Mall Parking Inefficiencies

Commercial shopping mall parking facilities face critical operational friction, driving customer dissatisfaction, space waste, and internal congestion.

• Search Time & Congestion

Drivers spend 10–25 minutes circulating through parking aisles, creating bottlenecks at main mall entrances, escalators, and access ramps.

• Inefficient Space Utilization

Conventional facilities require wide door-opening clearance, pedestrian walkways, and generous turning buffers, losing 30–40% of potential floor capacity.

• Limited Real-Time Visibility

Static signs provide coarse total counts but lack bay-level telemetry, predictive arrival forecasts, and verified reservation capabilities.

• Manual & Disconnected Operations

Traditional valet services rely on human drivers, manual key handoffs, liability risks, and fragmented operational systems without digital tracking.

Core Operational Friction: Conventional mall parking produces compound operational drag — customer frustration, lost retail dwell time, and severe structural spatial inefficiency.

Smart Services & Future Expansion Opportunities

FUTURE EXPANSION ROADMAP • NOT PART OF INITIAL BASELINE SCOPE
EXP 1 EV Charging Integration

Autonomous robots transport electric vehicles to a shared bank of DC fast chargers once parked. After reaching target charge, the robot relocates the car back to standard storage, eliminating EV bay hogging.

• Potential: 4x charger utilization efficiency
EXP 2 Automated Car Wash Bays

Customers opt into an automated exterior cleaning service via the mobile application. The fleet scheduler routes the vehicle through an automated wash tunnel during customer shopping dwell time before return.

• Revenue model: Value-added valet care service
EXP 3 Security & Vision Telematics

Stationary robotic patrolling and automated perimeter visual inspection. Optical sensors verify tire condition, exterior security, and body integrity while vehicles are stored in the facility.

• Security assurance: Zero unauthorized physical contact
EXP 4 Outdoor Valet Scaling (STAN)

Multi-facility expansion leveraging heavy-duty tow-arm robotics (Stanley Robotics STAN) designed for outdoor airport parking lots, open-air mall decks, and all-weather surface conditions.
